# Vendor and Contract Delivery

## Purpose
When suppliers deliver part of a solution, the workflow must make their milestones and the quality of their deliverables visible. A vendor acceptance checkpoint and a client user acceptance stage are the two standard controls.

## Vendor acceptance checkpoint
Before vendor deliverables are integrated, they are checked against contractual acceptance criteria: functional conformance, defect thresholds, documentation and security obligations. A supplier manager approves acceptance and records a conformance certificate. Rejected drops return to rework with findings shared with the vendor.

## Client user acceptance testing
When an external client commissions the work, payment milestones often depend on formal acceptance. The client tests in their own environment against agreed criteria and signs off through a guarded transition. Recording acceptance in the workflow avoids disputes over what counted as done.

## Fixed price contracts
Fixed price contracts usually fix scope, which favours a stage gate lifecycle with formal change control. Any change to scope, price or dates is raised as a change request.

## Signals in a charter
External vendors, suppliers, technology partners, fixed price contracts, commercial off the shelf packages, client acceptance and payment milestones.
