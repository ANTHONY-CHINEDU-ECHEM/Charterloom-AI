# Project Charter: Northern Compass

Domain: Public Sector. Business unit: Benefits Delivery.

## Purpose
The purpose of Northern Compass is to coordinate a programme that rebuilds the benefits eligibility checker and citizen account service across several product teams.

## Background
Delivery spans 7 teams across 3 locations with shared release dependencies, including two platform teams that own identity and payments. Cross team dependencies are the primary schedule risk identified by the programme office. Most requirements are known, with a handful expected to change after the first release.

## Objectives
* Increase digital adoption among members of the public to 75 percent within a year.
* Reduce average handling time for caseworkers by 25 percent.
* Retire 5 legacy components by the end of the programme.

## Scope
In scope: eligibility checking, the citizen account, notifications and caseworker views.
Out of scope: Changes to the enterprise identity provider. Organisational restructuring of the operations teams.

## Team and Stakeholders
The delivery team comprises 64 people organised into 7 teams. The planned duration is 60 weeks from mobilisation. Stakeholders include representatives from 9 departments with differing priorities.

## Constraints and Assumptions
The service will be used directly by members of the public and must work for people with a wide range of needs. The system will store personal data including names, contact details and account history. Peak load is forecast at 1,500 requests per second when award letters are issued.

## Regulatory and Compliance Context
The service must meet WCAG 2.2 AA and the Public Sector Bodies Accessibility Regulations. The solution falls within scope of UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
