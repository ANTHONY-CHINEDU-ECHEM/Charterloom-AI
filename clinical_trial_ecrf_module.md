# Project Charter: Meridian Capture

Domain: Pharmaceuticals and Life Sciences. Business unit: Clinical Operations. Initiative type: regulatory module.

## Purpose
The purpose of Meridian Capture is to implement an electronic case report form module for oncology trials that replaces paper source data verification at 140 trial sites.

## Background
The last regulatory inspection raised two audit findings about incomplete audit trails in the current data capture tool. The functional design was agreed in detail with clinical operations, data management and quality assurance, and the specification was baselined last quarter; changes require formal approval through the change control board.

## Objectives
* Close both inspection findings before the next inspection window.
* Reduce query resolution time for site data by 35 percent.
* Provide auditable evidence that obligations are met for every release.

## Scope
In scope: form design, edit checks, electronic signatures by investigators, query management and the audit trail.
Out of scope: Randomisation and trial supply management. Data warehouse reporting beyond the agreed operational extracts.

## Team and Stakeholders
The delivery team comprises 11 people organised into 2 teams, plus an independent validation lead from Quality Assurance. The planned duration is 36 weeks from mobilisation.

## Constraints and Assumptions
A fixed go live date of March 2027 has been set by the first patient visit of the next pivotal study. The system will store personal data including patient identifiers, adverse events and health information. Any downtime during site visits affects trial conduct.

## Regulatory and Compliance Context
The module is a validated system under FDA 21 CFR Part 11 electronic records and signatures, so every approval needs an electronic signature with its meaning recorded and a secure audit trail. Patient data is processed under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 and under equivalent EU obligations at European sites.
