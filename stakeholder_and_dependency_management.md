# Stakeholder and Dependency Management

## Stakeholder demonstrations
When many business owners share accountability, regular demonstrations of working software keep expectations aligned. A monthly stakeholder demonstration, separate from team level reviews, is recommended when more than four departments are involved or when executive sponsors need direct visibility.

Projects that show the product to stakeholders only at the end commonly face large late change requests and escalations to the sponsor.

## Dependency management
Dependencies on other teams, shared services and platforms are a leading cause of delay in multi team work. Recommended controls:
* Record a named dependency owner on every blocked item.
* Hold a weekly cross team dependency sync of thirty minutes.
* Escalate items blocked on another team for more than one working day.
* Visualise dependencies on a board that shows who needs what from whom and by when.

## Signals in a charter
Several teams, platform teams, shared release dependencies, integration with enterprise platforms, a programme office, several departments and executive sponsors.
