# Project Charter: Copper Relay

Domain: Energy and Utilities. Business unit: Smart Metering.

## Purpose
The purpose of Copper Relay is to certify and roll out new firmware for the smart meter communications hub across an installed base of 2.3 million devices, co developed with our hardware partner.

## Background
Firmware must be validated on physical devices before field deployment, and hardware prototypes arrive in batches, constraining when integration testing can start. Scope has been contractually fixed and signed off by the steering committee. Changes after tooling is cut are extremely expensive, so design decisions must be frozen early.

## Objectives
* Deploy certified firmware to 95 percent of the installed base within six months of approval.
* Reduce field visits caused by communications faults by 30 percent.
* Achieve zero bricked devices during rollout.

## Scope
In scope: firmware development, laboratory certification, staged over the air deployment and field monitoring.
Out of scope: Native tablet applications. Changes to third party contracts beyond those listed.

## Team and Stakeholders
The delivery team comprises 14 people organised into 2 teams, plus engineers from the hardware partner. The planned duration is 48 weeks from mobilisation.

## Constraints and Assumptions
Two suppliers deliver modules that must be accepted against contractual criteria. A fixed go live date of September 2027 has been set by an external obligation. Failures would directly affect domestic customers and be visible to the regulator.

## Regulatory and Compliance Context
Controls required under the NIS2 Directive apply to this service because smart metering forms part of essential energy infrastructure. Compliance with ISO/IEC 27001 information security controls must be demonstrated before go live.
